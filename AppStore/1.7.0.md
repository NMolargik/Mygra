## Description (4000 characters max)

Mygra: Your Intelligent Migraine Journal

Take control of your migraines with Mygra, a private, intelligent companion that helps you spot patterns, understand your triggers, and find relief. Log an attack in seconds, let on-device Apple Intelligence explain what may have contributed, and watch the trends emerge. Everything syncs securely through your own iCloud account and connects with Apple Health for the full picture of your well-being.

LOG MIGRAINES IN SECONDS
• Capture start and end times, pain and stress on a 0–10 scale, triggers from a curated list (or your own), foods, and notes.
• Track an attack as it happens: start a migraine, update its intensity as it changes, and follow it on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island with a Live Activity.
• Health and weather are attached automatically, so each entry records how you slept, what you drank, and what the sky was doing.
• Pin important episodes, tag them your way, and swipe to pin or delete.

INSIGHTS, PRIVATELY
• Migraine Assistant: chat with an analyst powered by Apple Intelligence that knows your history and profile. Every word stays on your device.
• Per-migraine insight: a plain-language explanation of likely contributing factors, generated on-device when you log an attack.
• Quick Bits: rule-based trends on frequency, severity, duration, common triggers, foods, hydration, sleep, glucose, oxygen, and how your pain tends to build or ease.
• Weather correlations: see how pressure, humidity, temperature, and conditions line up with your attacks, and get a heads-up when conditions turn risky.
• Apple Health integration: sleep, water, caffeine, food, steps, heart rate, blood oxygen, glucose, and menstrual phase feed your insights. Completed migraines are written back to Health as headaches.

SEE THE BIG PICTURE
• Dashboard: your migraine-free streak, local weather, today's Health stats with Quick Add for water, caffeine, food, and sleep, and the latest Quick Bits.
• Calendar: a month view colored by severity, with a per-day list and tag filters.
• History: searchable, grouped by month, with powerful filters by date range, minimum pain, triggers, and pinned status.
• Export a PDF report to share with your doctor.

EVERYWHERE YOU ARE
• Apple Watch: start or end a migraine from your wrist and see your streak on a complication.
• Home Screen widget with days since your last migraine.
• Siri and Shortcuts: start, end, check, or update a migraine without opening the app. Migraines appear in Spotlight.
• iPad and Mac: a sidebar, two-column layouts, keyboard shortcuts, and a menu bar.
• Available in English, Spanish, French (Canada), and Japanese.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your data lives on your device and in your personal iCloud. Mygra has no accounts, no servers, and no tracking. AI analysis never leaves your device.

Whether you live with chronic migraines or the occasional bad headache, Mygra helps you find answers, so you can spend less time in pain and more time living.

Mygra may use on-device intelligence to generate wellness insights. These are for informational purposes only and are not medical advice. Some features require iOS 26 or iPadOS 26; the Migraine Assistant and per-migraine insights require Apple Intelligence.
